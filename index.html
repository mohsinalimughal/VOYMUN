About VOYMUN

More than a conference. A platform for every voice.

Voice of Youth Model United Nations believes every student deserves an opportunity to express their ideas, develop leadership skills, and make their voice heard.

Our mission is to make Model United Nations accessible to everyone, especially talented and passionate students who may not have the financial means to attend expensive conferences.

Whether this is your first MUN or you are an experienced delegate, VOYMUN provides a platform to learn, collaborate, negotiate, and lead.

"To give every student the confidence, knowledge and ability to use their voice with purpose."

— The VOYMUN mission

Committees

Six committees. Countless perspectives.

From Pakistan's parliament to the Security Council, pick the room where your voice fits best.

PNA

Pakistan National Assembly

Parliamentary debate, national legislation, governance, and policy-making.

Agenda

The System Has Collapsed

Discussing the Structural Changes and Constitutional Amendment

FCC

Federal Constitutional Court of Pakistan

Constitutional interpretation, judicial reasoning, and landmark legal questions.

Agenda

A petition has been filed in the Federal Constitutional Court to challenge the Governor's Rule and Emergency in Khyber Pakhtunkhwa

UNSC

United Nations Security Council

International peace, security, conflict resolution, and global diplomacy.

Agenda

Beyond Borders

Preventing the Regionalisation of the Sudanese Civil War and Addressing External Involvement in the Conflict

UNHRC

United Nations Human Rights Council

Human rights, international accountability, and protection of fundamental freedoms.

Agenda

Breaking Point

Enforcing Accountability for War Crimes and Attacks on Civilian and Aid Infrastructure

UN Women

Gender Equality & Empowerment of Women

Gender equality, women's empowerment, and global development.

Agenda

Shadow Markets

Combating Human Trafficking, Forced Marriages, and Modern Slavery Among Displaced Women

SPECPOL

Special Political and Decolonization Committee

Political challenges, decolonization, territorial disputes, and international cooperation.

Agenda

Addressing the border dispute and their effects as a critical escalation point in the Israel–Palestine crisis (both the West Bank and Gaza)
